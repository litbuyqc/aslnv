03 / Oopbuy shopping guide

A better shortlist starts with better notes.

When reviewing Oopbuy finds or building an Oopbuy product list, record the exact variant alongside your link. A useful comparison includes measurements, material information, the date you checked the listing, and costs that still need confirmation.

Start browsing CAFANS ↗

  1. Choose the use before the look.

    Decide whether you need an everyday shoe, a warm layer, sportswear, or an accessory. Narrow the category before comparing individual items.

  2. Match the exact variant.

    Record the color, size, material description, and any model reference. Photos of another variant are not a reliable substitute.

  3. Compare measurements and images.

    Use the Oopbuy size guide checklist below. If quality-control images are available, review the selected item’s visible details and measurements.

  4. Confirm the total before deciding.

    Separate the item price from delivery, service charges, international shipping, and any applicable import costs.

Before you open a listing

Straightforward answers.

Use these answers alongside the current product listing and the terms of the service handling your order.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize item links, categories, prices, sizing information, and quality-control notes. A listing is a starting point for comparison, not proof of availability, authenticity, or quality.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a category directory and a shopping checklist. Its category links open CAFANS; it does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet or a live Oopbuy inventory feed.

What should I check before choosing a product?

Check the exact item variant, measurements, material description, current listing details, return conditions, and estimated total cost. If quality-control images are available, compare them with the selected variant.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Compare the listing’s measurements with an item that already fits. For clothing, check chest width, length, waist, and inseam as applicable. For shoes, compare foot length and the specific listing’s size chart rather than relying only on a regional size label.

Are shipping costs included in a product list price?

Do not assume they are included. Check item cost, domestic delivery, any applicable service charges, international shipping, and destination-specific taxes or duties before deciding.

Is this an official Oopbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent shopping guide linking to CAFANS. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, or Hermès. Brand images are editorial references and do not establish CAFANS stock or authenticity.
